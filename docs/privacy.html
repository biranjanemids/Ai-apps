Privacy Policy

OneCartBot · Last updated: July 2026

OneCartBot ("we", "us") is a WhatsApp-based shopping assistant that helps you search for products and compare prices across Indian e-commerce platforms. This policy explains what information we process when you use the service, and how we handle it.

1. Information we process

  • WhatsApp phone number — provided automatically by WhatsApp when you message us; used only to reply to you.
  • Message content — your search queries and taps on buttons, used to find products and respond to your requests.
  • Session data — your recent search results, saved wishlist items, and language preference, kept temporarily so the conversation flows naturally.
  • Delivery details you choose to share — if you use the order-summary feature and share an address or contact number, it is used only to compose your order summary message and is not stored after your session ends.
Short version: session data is held in temporary memory and is automatically deleted after 30 minutes of inactivity. We do not maintain a permanent database of your conversations.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not process payments or collect payment card details. All purchases are completed on the retailer's own website or app.
  • We do not send you marketing messages unless you have messaged us first or opted in (for example, by saving an item to your wishlist for price-drop alerts).
  • We do not share your identity with retailers or affiliate networks. Affiliate links contain no personal information about you.

3. Affiliate links

Product links we share may be affiliate links. If you make a qualifying purchase through one, the retailer may pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Anonymous, aggregate click statistics (for example, "how many taps went to each store") are used to operate and improve the service. These statistics are not linked to your identity when shared with anyone.

4. Third-party services

The service relies on the following third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • WhatsApp / Meta Platforms — message delivery (WhatsApp Privacy Policy)
  • Product data providers — product search results are retrieved from public shopping data sources; your phone number is never sent to them.
  • Retailers (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Nykaa, Ajio, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart) — when you tap a buy link, you leave OneCartBot and the retailer's terms and privacy policy apply.

5. Data retention

  • Conversation session data: deleted automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: retained in aggregate form only, without personal identifiers.
  • Server logs: kept briefly for security and debugging, then rotated out.

6. Your rights

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), you may ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent at any time. Because session data expires automatically within 30 minutes, in most cases there is nothing left to delete — but you can always contact us to confirm. You can also stop all processing simply by blocking the WhatsApp number.

7. Children

The service is intended for users aged 18 and above, consistent with WhatsApp's own terms of service.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
